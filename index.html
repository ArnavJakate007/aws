SkinSight AI

A computer-vision web app that analyses facial skin from a photo. It grades acne severity, detects lesions, segments the forehead, cheeks and jawline, and tracks progress over time.

03

GridEV

Intelligent EV-charging infrastructure planning for Indian cities. It uses LSTM demand forecasting, RL-based load balancing and ML site scoring on a hex grid, and is containerised for production.

LSTMRLNext.jsGoDocker
04

Nexus

An ML-first skill-gap and learning-path engine. A five-layer pipeline uses spaCy NER, ONNX embeddings, an O*NET graph with Dijkstra and an optional local LLM. It's fully containerised with Docker and nginx.

spaCyONNXPythonDockernginx

sattsr

Temporal super-resolution for geostationary thermal imagery. A learned optical-flow interpolation model raises cadence from 30 to 15 and 7.5 minutes, and it's applied to ISRO's INSAT-3DR/3DS satellites.
